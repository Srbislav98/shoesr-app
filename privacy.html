Privacy

Last updated September 27, 2026

ShoesR keeps what you do in the app on your phone: your filters, the shoes you swiped and the shoes you saved.

Accounts

Accounts are optional. If you create one, your email address and your saved shoes are stored with our backend provider, Supabase, so they can follow you to another phone. Swipes are stored with your account to improve recommendations.

Shopping links

Shop links open the retailer through an affiliate network (impact.com), which records the click so the retailer can credit the sale. The retailer's own privacy policy applies on their site or app.

Ads and crash reports

Free users see ads from Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising identifier, subject to the consent choices you make in the app. Crash reports are sent to Firebase Crashlytics so we can fix bugs.

Deleting your data

Delete your account at any time from Profile in the app. That removes your account data from our backend. Data kept only on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app.
